## Definitions

- **CRE / 中欧班列**（中欧班列）: China-Europe block trains — rail CTU load distribution rules differ from ocean but share overweight/bias concerns.
- **偏载偏重**（偏载偏重）: Uneven or concentrated load — may force restow/delay; published public fine schedules scarce.
